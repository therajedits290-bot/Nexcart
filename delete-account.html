Delete your account

Request deletion of your Nexcart account and the personal data we hold about you.

How to request deletion

Email us from the mobile number registered to your account. Use the button below — it opens your mail app with the subject and the details we need already filled in.

Request account deletion

Or write to gunaraj88@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Nexcart account”.

What we delete

  • Your account: name, mobile number and password
  • If you signed in with Google, the Google name, email and account identifier we stored
  • Your saved delivery addresses
  • Your cart and wishlist on our servers
  • Any refund details you gave us (UPI ID or bank account details)

What we may keep, and why

  • Order and tax records. Invoices and order history may be kept for the period Indian tax and accounting law requires. These are kept only for compliance and are not used for anything else.
  • Records needed to resolve an open dispute — an unfinished return, refund or complaint — until it is closed.

We will tell you which of these applies when we confirm your deletion.

How long it takes

We verify the request against the registered mobile number and complete deletion within 30 days. You will get a confirmation email when it is done.

On your own phone

Separately, you can clear everything the app stored on your device at any time: Android Settings → Apps → Nexcart → Storage → Clear data, or simply uninstall the app. That removes your local cart, wishlist, saved addresses and the cached images.

Just want to log out instead?

You do not need to delete your account to stop using the app. Open Profile → Logout, and delete your account later if you wish. For anything else, see our Privacy Policy.
