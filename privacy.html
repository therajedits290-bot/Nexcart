Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

This policy explains what Nexcart collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It covers the Nexcart mobile app and website.

1. Who we are

Nexcart is operated by Nexcart, Tamil Nadu, India. For any privacy question, or to exercise a right set out below, write to gunaraj88@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Account details. When you create an account we collect your name, mobile number and a password. If you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and Google account identifier instead.

Order and delivery details. The name, mobile number and delivery address (with city, state and PIN code) you enter at checkout, plus the items ordered, the amount and the order status.

Refund details — only if you ask for a refund. The UPI ID you give us, or your account holder name, bank account number, IFSC code and bank name. We use these only to pay the refund.

Your activity in the app. Cart, wishlist, saved addresses, recently viewed items and order history. These live on your device and, for account holders, on our servers.

Basic technical data. As with any website, our servers receive your IP address and basic device and browser information when the app talks to them.

3. What we do NOT collect

  • We never ask for or store card numbers, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking passwords — payment is Cash on Delivery.
  • We do not read your SMS. There is no OTP; we send nothing by SMS.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.

4. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in
  • To accept, pack, ship and deliver your orders, and to show you tracking
  • To process cancellations, returns and refunds
  • To answer your support questions
  • To keep catalogue, stock and pricing accurate
  • To detect and prevent fraud and misuse
  • To meet our legal and tax obligations

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

  • Delivery partners — your name, address and phone number, so your order can reach you.
  • Our hosting provider — the service that stores your account and orders for us.
  • Google — only if you choose “Continue with Google”, to verify your sign-in.
  • Image delivery network — product photos are served from a content delivery network, which sees your IP address.
  • Law enforcement — where we are legally required to disclose.

6. How long we keep it, and how we protect it

We keep your account data while your account is open. Order and refund records are kept longer where tax and accounting law requires it. Data held on your phone (cart, wishlist, cached images) stays until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Access to customer data inside our systems is limited to the people who need it to run the store.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and correct your name and addresses in the app at any time. You may also ask us to:

  • Show you the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is inaccurate
  • Delete your account and personal data
  • Withdraw a consent you gave us earlier
Deleting your account. Write to gunaraj88@gmail.com from the mobile number registered to your account, or use the account deletion request page. We act on verified requests within 30 days. We may keep order and tax records for as long as the law requires, and we will tell you when we do.

8. Children

Nexcart is meant for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us their data, contact gunaraj88@gmail.com and we will delete it.

9. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking. The app stores a small amount of data on your device — cart, wishlist, saved addresses, your sign-in token and a cache of product images — so that it opens quickly and works on a weak signal. Clearing the app's data removes all of it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data we will update this page and the “last updated” date. Important changes will be shown in the app.
